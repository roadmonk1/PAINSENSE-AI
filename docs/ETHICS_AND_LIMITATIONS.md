# Ethics, Safety Boundaries & System Limitations

**System Version:** 1.2.0-Enterprise  
**Project:** PAINSENSE-AI — Multimodal Pain Detection & Healthcare Assistance System  

---

## 1. Ethical Charter & Non-Negotiable Boundaries

PAINSENSE-AI is explicitly built as an **accessibility-first assistive communication and pain-monitoring tool**. It is **NOT** a medical diagnostic device, does **NOT** practice medicine, and does **NOT** possess independent clinical decision authority.

### Core Non-Negotiable Principles:
1. **Assistive Communication, Not Diagnosis:**  
   The system facilitates patient-provider communication. It cannot diagnose disease etiologies, prescribe therapies, or alter medication regimens.
2. **Primacy of Patient Self-Report:**  
   Patient self-report is the clinical gold standard. Passive computational sensors (e.g. computer vision detecting relaxed facial muscles) are strictly prohibited from discounting or overriding explicit patient reports of severe distress.
3. **Human-in-the-Loop Clinical Oversight:**  
   No emergency dispatch, hospital admission, or invasive clinical action may be initiated autonomously by the software. All outputs require validation by a licensed healthcare professional.
4. **Zero Fabrication of Clinical Validation:**  
   The system explicitly states that automated test suites are based on synthetic benchmarks and public research targets (UNBC-McMaster, Torgo, WLASL, INCLUDE). We do not claim completed FDA/CE-mark certified clinical trials.

## 2. Transparent Labeling Standards

To prevent algorithmic confusion or over-reliance by clinicians and caregivers, every data field across the API, frontend UI, and database records is explicitly tagged:

| Label Tag | Context & Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| `[USER REPORT]` | Ground-truth subjective input provided directly by the patient (verbal, touch-screen, or typed). |
| `[USER SIGNED STATEMENT]` | Sign language communication verified and confirmed by the patient. |
| `[ALGORITHMIC SIGNAL]` | Raw biometric extraction (e.g., PSPI facial action unit tension, acoustic jitter/strain). |
| `[AI OBSERVATION]` | Inferred passive telemetry generated by computer vision or audio classifiers. |
| `[HEURISTIC]` | Deterministic clinical rules (e.g., pain score $\ge 8/10 \rightarrow$ urgent flag). |
| `[SYSTEM RECOMMENDATION]` | Supportive triage priority (`routine`, `caution`, `urgent`, `emergency`) intended for nurse review. |
| `[DEMO / SIMULATION]` | Simulated telephonic dispatch or synthetic benchmark evaluation. |

## 3. Algorithmic Bias, Inequity & Physical Limitations

### 3.1 Computer Vision & Facial Analysis
- **Skin Pigmentation (Fitzpatrick I–VI):** Geometric landmark tracking can experience reduced contrast sensitivity under suboptimal illumination on dark skin tones. Mitigated by dynamic contrast normalization and lighting quality warnings.
- **Neurological & Physical Variations:** Patients with unilateral facial paralysis (Bell's palsy), acute stroke, Parkinsonian masked facies, or severe facial trauma cannot be evaluated via standard PSPI Action Unit metrics. The system must be placed in self-report / sign-language mode.
- **Surgical Masks & Cannulas:** Occlusion of lower facial landmarks elevates model uncertainty rather than generating false severe pain predictions.

### 3.2 Voice & Acoustic Processing
- **Dysarthria & Speech Impairments:** Severe respiratory fatigue or dysarthria alters fundamental frequency and speech cadence. The acoustic classifier measures vocal strain as a non-diagnostic physiological signal.
- **Language & Accents:** Entity extraction currently maps English pain descriptors. Regional colloquialisms require localized terminology adaptation.

### 3.3 Sign Language Communication
- **Dialect Disparities:** American Sign Language (ASL) and Indian Sign Language (ISL) exhibit different syntax and vocabulary. Cross-dialect confusion is mitigated by providing an explicit dialect toggle.
- **Physical Hand Deformities:** Arthritic contractures, missing digits, or tremors reduce landmark classification confidence. The system provides an interactive touch-screen correction modal.

## 4. Privacy, Autonomy & Consent

1. **Granular Consent Controls:** Patients retain the absolute right to revoke camera or microphone access at any time via the Privacy Center (`/settings`).
2. **Local-Only Processing Mode:** Activates in-browser MediaPipe inference with zero transmission of raw biometric coordinates to cloud servers.
3. **Data Portability (GDPR Art. 20):** Patients can download their complete clinical assessment archive in JSON format with a single click.
4. **Right to Erasure (GDPR Art. 17):** One-click data deletion removes all database records associated with the user account.
